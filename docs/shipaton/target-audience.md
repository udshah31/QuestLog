# QuestLog — Target audience and content

Play Console → *Policy and programs → App content → Target audience and content*.

## Step 1 — Target age

| Age group | Tick? |
|---|---|
| 5 and under | No |
| 6–8 | No |
| 9–12 | No |
| 13–15 | No (optional — see below) |
| 16–17 | No (optional — see below) |
| **18 and over** | **Yes** |

**Recommended: 18 and over only.** QuestLog sells a subscription and sends typed text to an AI
service; an adult audience keeps the review simple.

Including **13–17** is allowed and honest if you want teens (digital detox suits them), but:
- Play reviews teen-targeted apps more closely for ads/data practices (QuestLog has no ads —
  fine), and
- the store listing and privacy policy must suit teens (they already do).

**Never tick an under-13 group** — that puts QuestLog under the Families policy (stricter ad,
data and SDK rules — only Families-certified SDKs).

## Step 2 — Appeal to children

*"Could your store listing unintentionally appeal to children?"* → **No**

Why it's honest: the listing uses a flat editorial style (charcoal on paper, one red accent, a
serif wordmark), no cartoon characters or mascots, and copy about screen time, focus and
subscriptions aimed at adults. The "realm" is abstract blocks, not a kids' game world.

## Step 3 — Store presence

Nothing else to change: no "Designed for Families" opt-in, no teacher-approved programme.

## Consistency check

| Where | Must say |
|---|---|
| Content rating | Everyone / PEGI 3 (`content-rating.md`) — a low rating is fine for an 18+ audience; it describes content, not audience |
| Privacy policy | "not directed at children under 13" (`docs/privacy.md`) ✔ |
| Data safety | no data from children collected knowingly ✔ |
